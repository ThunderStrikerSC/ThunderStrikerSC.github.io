AT-HOME TRAINING

20-MINUTE
BALL MASTERY

A ball, a little space and twenty minutes. This is optional work for any Thunder Striker who wants extra touches at home.

20 MINUTES • NO EQUIPMENT BEYOND A BALL
01 • WARM UP

Get the feet moving.

Three straight minutes. Keep the ball close and stay light on your feet.

1 MIN

Toe Taps

Alternate feet on top of the ball. Find a steady rhythm, then speed it up.

1 MIN

Bells / Foundations

Pass the ball quickly between the insides of your feet. Small, controlled touches.

1 MIN

Inside-Outside Touches

Use the inside then outside of the same foot. Switch feet halfway through.

02 • BALL MASTERY

Six moves. Twelve minutes.

For each move: work for 90 seconds, then take 30 seconds to reset. Quality first. Speed comes after control.

2 MIN

Sole Rolls

Roll the ball across your body with the sole. Receive it with the opposite foot and repeat.

2 MIN

Push-Pulls

Pull the ball back with the sole, then push it forward with the inside or laces. Alternate feet.

2 MIN

V-Pulls

Pull the ball toward you, then push it diagonally away to make a V shape. Work both feet.

2 MIN

L-Turns

Pull the ball back with the sole and move it across your body behind the standing leg.

2 MIN

Scissors

Circle a foot around the front of the ball, then explode the other direction with the outside touch.

2 MIN

Stepovers

Step over and around the ball, sell the fake with your body, then take the ball away under control.

03 • FINISH

Five minutes to own the ball.

2 MIN

Weak Foot Only

Pick any moves from above, but only use your weaker foot.

2 MIN

Build a Combo

Connect two or three moves together. Example: sole roll → scissors → outside touch. Make your own.

1 MIN

60-Second Challenge

Go. Quick, clean touches for one full minute. Keep control and finish stronger than you started.

THE STANDARD
NOBODY IS CHECKING.

You do not have to do this. But every extra touch builds comfort and confidence on the ball. Twenty minutes today is twenty minutes of work you did not have yesterday.
