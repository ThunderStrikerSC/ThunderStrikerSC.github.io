Thunder Strikers Battle to 2–2 Draw in Season Opener

THUNDER STRIKERS 2 • SAC WOLFPACK 2

Severn Danza Park • Fall 2026

The Thunder Strikers opened their Fall 2026 season Friday night with a hard-fought 2–2 draw against SAC U12B Wolfpack in a matchup between two evenly matched teams.

The Strikers settled into the game well and began finding success through their passing and combination play. Midway through the first half, that pressure paid off when Zeke found the back of the net to give Thunder Strikers a 1–0 advantage.

SAC responded before halftime, leveling the match and sending the teams into the break tied 1–1.

The Wolfpack carried some momentum into the second half and eventually took a 2–1 lead. The Strikers, however, never looked like a team that believed the game was over. They continued to defend, push forward and search for an equalizer.

It did not take long for that response to come. Langston scored the tying goal in the second half to bring the Strikers back level at 2–2. From there, both teams continued to battle. Thunder Strikers created opportunities to find a winner and came close on several occasions, while the defense held strong at the other end.

Neither side was able to break through again, and the final whistle brought an entertaining opening-night battle to a close at 2–2.

For the Strikers, the result was an encouraging start to a season centered on development. The passing, communication and willingness to keep playing after falling behind were all positive signs from a team beginning its next chapter.
